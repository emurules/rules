# Changelog

All notable changes to EMU Rules are documented here.

## v2026.3 Edition

A clarity-and-consistency pass. Every point below was raised by cross-checking
the ruleset against real judging practice and against the FIIM text; the
document's own preamble requires such fixes to be made in the Rules themselves,
under a new version.

### New Part

- **PART XVI — Gesture Reference** added. PARTS IV and IX repeatedly referred to
  "the official gesture established by these Rules" without any clause, figure,
  or table describing those gestures. PART XVI now describes each one in words:
  the Sheriff sign, the Don sign, the two Don-check results, the two
  Sheriff-check results, and the shot-miming motion. The references in §4.2,
  §4.3, §4.4, §4.5, §9.2, and §9.3–§9.6 now point to it.

### Gameplay clarifications

- **§2.5** — a game now counts for rating by default; a non-rating or tournament
  game must be designated as such before the first Night.
- **§9.2** — during role distribution the Judge now gives the role gesture with
  each card: a raised thumb for a Red card, a lowered thumb for a Dark card, and
  the Sheriff or Don sign for the special roles. The previous clause forbidding
  any gesture beyond showing the card is removed.
- **§4.5 / §9.5** — the Sheriff check result is now a hand gesture only (thumb up
  for Red, thumb down for Dark). The mandatory head-movement component is
  removed. The Don check result keeps its head movement.
- **§4.3.5 / §9.6** — the shot-miming motion is described plainly: two fingers of
  a raised hand held high, timed to the called seat number, direction
  immaterial. A misfire still results only from firing on a different number,
  firing on more than one number, or not firing.
- **§4.6** — the Best Move right is stated to attach to whichever player is first
  killed by a Night shot, whatever Night that falls on; an intervening misfire
  does not extinguish it.
- **§4.7 / §6.3** — win-condition checks use the moment a vote or Night kill is
  *determined*, not the player's physical departure or final speech.
- **§5.8 (Split Vote)** — reworded. Each re-vote is among the players currently
  tied; the narrow-and-re-vote step repeats only while the tied set keeps
  shrinking (it always terminates: the set strictly shrinks and cannot fall
  below two); the eliminate-all question and its effect are about the players
  *currently tied*, not the original nomination list; a non-vote in a re-vote
  falls on the last-nominated player still tied. The "three at nine" / "all
  players" carve-outs are now a pointer to §8.6.
- **§8.1** — where a removal cancels the vote, the rest of the Day still runs and
  only the vote is skipped. Where the removed player was themselves the leading
  candidate, the Judge records the vote as determined at the moment of removal.
- **§8.5** — unchanged in substance; a note confirms that a player leaving for
  any reason, including a removal, resets the three-Night draw count.

### Consistency

- **§8.4** reduced to a pointer to §12.4, which holds the full statement of the
  three-Warning speech rules (previously stated in both places in slightly
  different words).
- **§13.7.4** now cites §12.7.5 and §12.7.10 instead of the phrase "profanity or
  insults", which matched no foul definition.
- **§12.10 ↔ §13.11** cross-reference each other so the Notice and the
  Disciplinary penalty are not conflated.
- **§8.6** notes that its restrictions are restated at §5.8.7.

### Severity reclassifications

- **Concealing speech information in another language** moved from a
  Disqualifying Foul to a Warning (now §12.6.10).
- **Crying at the gaming table** is no longer an automatic Disqualifying Foul.
  New **§12.11** makes an involuntary emotional response a Judge-discretion
  matter: Notice, then Warning if it disrupts play, and removal only if play
  cannot continue.
- **Night mask contact** — §12.6.9 (a Warning) is narrowed to Relaxed Seating
  and the Day. A new Disqualifying Foul (§12.7.7) covers mask contact during the
  Night where the eyes are or could be exposed.

### §12.7 renumbering

Removing two items and inserting one renumbers the Disqualifying Fouls list:

| Old | Content | New |
|---|---|---|
| §12.7.1–6 | peeking; non-game methods; unauthorised gestures; revealing role info; offensive/abusive language; intentional Night-procedure violation | §12.7.1–6 (unchanged) |
| — | **new** — Night mask contact with eyes exposed/exposable | §12.7.7 |
| §12.7.7 | any action compromising integrity | §12.7.8 |
| §12.7.8 | religious/ethical/non-game appeals | §12.7.9 |
| §12.7.9 | concealing speech info in a foreign language | removed → §12.6.10 (now a Warning) |
| §12.7.10 | crying at the table | removed → §12.11 (Judge discretion) |
| §12.7.11 | obscene language | §12.7.10 |
| §12.7.12 | use of "suicide" | §12.7.11 |
| §12.7.13 | leaving the table without permission | §12.7.12 |

### Divergences from FIIM

`DIVERGENCES.md` gains rows for the foreign-language downgrade, the crying
reclassification, the Night mask bright line, the Sheriff-check gesture (EMU
drops the head movement FIIM keeps), and the role-distribution gesture (EMU has
the Judge show the role gesture with each card). The §5.8 and §8.5 changes are
clarifications of FIIM's own wording, not divergences.

## v2026.2 Edition
- **1.2 Scope of Application** rewritten. The section previously held eight
  venue, equipment, and player-conduct clauses under a heading that never
  described their content. All eight were already restated in Part II, so
  the list is removed and 1.2 now states the actual scope of the Rules:
  what format they govern, when they apply, and how they interact with an
  event's own regulations.
- **2.3.3 Judge Equipment** removed. Its sole clause duplicated 2.1.3; the
  one distinct point ("at all times") is folded into 2.1.3.
- No section renumbering. 2.3 now contains 2.3.1 (Masks) and 2.3.2 (Music).
