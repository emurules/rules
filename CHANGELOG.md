# Changelog

All notable changes to EMU Rules are documented here.

## v2026.2 Edition
- **1.2 Scope of Application** rewritten. The section previously held eight
  venue, equipment, and player-conduct clauses under a heading that never
  described their content. All eight were already restated in Part II, so
  the list is removed and 1.2 now states the actual scope of the Rules:
  what format they govern, when they apply, and how they interact with an
  event's own regulations.
- **2.3.3 Judge Equipment** removed. Its sole clause duplicated 2.1.3; the
  one distinct point ("at all times") is folded into 2.1.3.
- No section renumbering. 2.3 now contains 2.3.1 (Masks) and 2.3.2 (Music).

## v2026.1 Edition
- Initial public edition of EMU Rules — English Mafia Unified.
- Renamed from v1.0 (2026 Edition) to v2026.1 to follow the new versioning
  convention: `v` followed by year, followed by release number. See
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md#versioning).
