## How decisions are made

EMU Rules is developed as an open, contribution-based project. Anyone may
propose a change by opening an issue or pull request; the Committee reviews
and discusses each proposal on its own merits before deciding whether to
merge it.

Every change to the Rules goes through the same process — issue or pull
request, review, discussion, approval — with no direct changes to the
published text, and every merged change is reflected in a version update.
See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to propose a change.
