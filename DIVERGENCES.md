# Divergences from FIIM

## What FIIM Is

FIIM (The Federation of Intellectual Games "Mafia") is the international governing body that publishes the original Russian-language rules — the equivalent of FIDE for chess or the ITF for tennis. EMU is an independent, English-language ruleset that is **FIIM-compatible**. EMU is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by FIIM.

## The short version

If you already know how to play under FIIM, you already know how to play EMU. Sit down and play — the day/night cycle, the roles, voting, checks, gestures, and win conditions are all the same game. Nothing about how you actually play changes.

A few words changed, and a handful of judge-and-scoring-level details differ in ways most players will never hit at the table. Both are below.

## Words that changed

Same game, some relabelling:

* **Black team** → **Dark team**
* The old disciplinary ladder gets a fourth name slotted in: **Notice → Warning → Disqualifying Foul → PPK** (four tiers existed before too — this just gives the lowest one its own name instead of overloading "Warning").
* **FIIM SK** → **EMU Judging Committee** (SK is Russian for "Judging Committee" — the name carries over in meaning, not just letters)
* **PPK** stays **PPK** — same abbreviation, same meaning. EMU spells it out as **Victory of the Opposite Team**, the term used at the table.
* **The Don's Night call** — FIIM: "Don wakes and looks for the Sheriff". EMU: **"Don wakes and does their check."** The word "Sheriff" is left out so that the real Sheriff is not tempted to open their eyes on the Don's call. Same moment, same check.
* **Night numbering starts at zero.** The Night of role distribution, the Dark team's agreement and the Sheriff's identification is **Night 0**, and the first shooting Night is **Night 1** (FIIM counts these as the first and second game Night). Same Nights, same procedure — only the count shifts by one. **Watch the words:** in EMU, "First Night" means Night 1, the first *shooting* Night, not the agreement Night as in FIIM.

## What actually changed (rule-wise)

Everything not listed here works exactly as it does under FIIM. These are the only places where the actual rule content is different, not just the label.

### At the table

| Situation | Under FIIM | Under EMU |
|---|---|---|
| Official language | Russian, plus an approved regional language | Whichever single language the tournament organiser designates — English by default, no approval step |
| Mask handling | Any night-time contact with the mask is a "night touch" disqualifying foul | Removing or handling the mask without being told to is a Warning during the Day or Relaxed Seating; a disqualifying foul at Night only where the eyes are, or could be, exposed |
| Concealing speech in another language | Automatic disqualifying foul; committed after the player's kill is announced or after they are voted out, it becomes PPK | A Warning instead — removal only if the Warnings add up, and never escalated to PPK |
| Crying at the table | Automatic disqualifying foul; committed after the player's kill is announced or after they are voted out, it becomes PPK | No automatic foul — the Judge gives a Notice first, and a disqualifying foul only if it keeps disrupting play after that. Never escalated to PPK |
| Voting with the wrong hand shape | Named, automatic disqualifying foul | A Warning instead — covered by the existing "breaking the established voting procedure" trigger |
| Involuntary (accidental) peeking | Named, automatic disqualifying foul | A Warning instead — only *deliberate* peeking is a disqualifying foul under EMU |
| Sheriff check result | The Judge shows a head movement together with the thumb (turn + thumb-up for Red, nod + thumb-down for Dark) | The thumb alone — up for Red, down for Dark, no head movement. The Don check result still uses a head movement |
| Role distribution | The Judge shows the card | The Judge also gives the role gesture with the card — thumb up for a Red card, thumb down for a Dark card, the Sheriff or Don sign for the special roles |
| Three-Night draw | "Three Nights in a row" — the text does not say whether the agreement Night counts | Only shooting Nights count (Night 1 onward); the agreement Night (Night 0) never counts |
| Best Move: player says something other than seat numbers | Only the numbers said before the interruption are recorded, and are scored normally on however many of them are correct. Asking for help with the Best Move is no longer a foul | Only seat numbers may be said. Anything else, including asking anyone at the table or in the audience for help, voids the bonus entirely — no points, even if some of the numbers were correct. One or two numbers stated within the time still score, the missing ones counting as wrong |
| Best Move after an earlier departure | No Best Move only where two or more players left by the Day 1 vote; a single Day 1 vote-out does not affect it | A Best Move only where the killed player is the first player to leave the game. Any earlier vote-out or removal, a single Day 1 vote-out included, means no Best Move. The player is still first-killed for Compensation Points and the tie-break |
| Who is "first-killed" (Best Move, Compensation Points) | Only a player killed on the first shooting Night. If that Night is a misfire, nobody in the game is first-killed | The first player killed in the game, whatever Night it happens on. If Night 1 is a misfire and the first kill comes on Night 2, the player killed on Night 2 is first-killed |

### Judge, discipline, and scoring

| Situation | Under FIIM | Under EMU |
|---|---|---|
| Judge bonus pool ceiling (total) | Can exceed 3.5 points with SK sign-off | Capped at 3.5 with Head Judge approval — no further escalation |
| Sheriff's missed or wasted check, once the Dark team is known (Small penalty) | Excused once "the Dark team has been collected" — the term is not defined | Excused once the Sheriff's checks, alone or with the count of players left, already establish every remaining player's team. This expressly covers deducing the last Dark player by elimination, not only checking all three |
| Sheriff not checking for two or more Nights (Big penalty) | No exception — applies even after the Dark team has been collected | Nights after the Dark team is known, in the sense of the row above, are not counted |
| One action meeting several penalty triggers | Not addressed in the penalty list; the general rule that sanctions for different points add up can be read to apply | One penalty only: the highest of the triggers met. They are never added together |
| Appeals | Can be filed as a protocol note or a written protest; a judge-conduct complaint is always available | Written appeals only; judge-conduct complaints are conditional on the tournament's own regulations |

EMU also writes its official gestures out in words (PART XVI) where FIIM gives them
as reference images. The intended gestures are the same; only the Sheriff-check
head movement, listed above, actually differs.

That's the whole list. If a situation isn't in this table, EMU handles it the same way FIIM does.
