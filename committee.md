# EMU Committee

The EMU Committee maintains the [EMU Rules](?page=rules) and oversees their ongoing development.

## Mission

English Mafia Unified exists to give English-speaking clubs and tournaments one clean set of rules — a single source of truth, not a patchwork of translations, house variants, and word-of-mouth clarifications. The Rules are written to stand on their own: no supplementary commentary, no separate interpretations maintained elsewhere. Where something needs to change, it changes in the text itself, under a new version number.

EMU is built to be FIIM-compatible. It is an independent, English-language ruleset — not a translation, and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by FIIM — but one written so that anyone who knows how to play under FIIM already knows how to play under EMU. See [Adapt in Your Club](?page=adapt-in-your-club) for the full list of divergences.

## How Decisions Are Made

EMU tracks FIIM. When FIIM releases an update, the Committee reviews it, discusses it, and decides whether it should be incorporated into the EMU Rules.

A changelog is kept, and the version number reflects whatever has changed. Every change to the Rules goes through the same process — Pull Request, Review, Discussion, Approval — with no direct changes to the published text. The full history of every change is preserved.
