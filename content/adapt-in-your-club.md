## What FIIM Is

FIIM (The Federation of Intellectual Games "Mafia") is the international governing body that publishes the original Russian-language rules — the equivalent of FIDE for chess or the ITF for tennis. EMU is an independent, English-language ruleset that is **FIIM-compatible**. EMU is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by FIIM.

## The short version

If you already know how to play under FIIM, you already know how to play EMU. Sit down and play — the day/night cycle, the roles, voting, checks, gestures, and win conditions are all the same game. Nothing about how you actually play changes.

A few words changed, and a handful of judge-and-scoring-level details differ in ways most players will never hit at the table. Both are below.

## Words that changed

Same game, some relabelling:

* **Black team** → **Dark team**
* The old disciplinary ladder gets a fourth name slotted in: **Notice → Warning → Disqualifying Foul → PPK** (four tiers existed before too — this just gives the lowest one its own name instead of overloading "Warning").
* **FIIM SK** → **EMU Judging Committee** (SK is Russian for "Judging Committee" — the name carries over in meaning, not just letters)
* **PPK** stays **PPK** — same abbreviation, same meaning.

## What actually changed (rule-wise)

Everything not listed here works exactly as it does under FIIM. These are the only places where the actual rule content is different, not just the label.

### At the table

| Situation | Under FIIM | Under EMU |
|---|---|---|
| Official language | Russian, plus an approved regional language | Whichever single language the tournament organiser designates — English by default, no approval step |
| Mask handling | Refusing to remove your mask when the Judge asks | Handling your mask improperly without being told to |
| Voting with the wrong hand shape | Named, automatic disqualifying foul | A Warning instead — covered by the existing "breaking the established voting procedure" trigger |
| Involuntary (accidental) peeking | Named, automatic disqualifying foul | A Warning instead — only *deliberate* peeking is a disqualifying foul under EMU |
| Best Move: player says something beyond the three numbers | Only the numbers said before the interruption are recorded, and are scored normally on however many of them are correct | The bonus is void entirely — no points, even if some of the truncated numbers were correct |

### Judge, discipline, and scoring

| Situation | Under FIIM | Under EMU |
|---|---|---|
| Sanctions after the result is decided | Mostly stop once the outcome is settled — only interfering with the leaving player's farewell speech still draws a foul | The Judge can keep sanctioning more broadly after the result is already decided |
| Judge bonus pool ceiling (total) | Can exceed 3.5 points with SK sign-off | Capped at 3.5 with Head Judge approval — no further escalation |
| Appeals | Can be filed as a protocol note or a written protest; a judge-conduct complaint is always available | Written appeals only; judge-conduct complaints are conditional on the tournament's own regulations |

That's the whole list. If a situation isn't in this table, EMU handles it the same way FIIM does.
